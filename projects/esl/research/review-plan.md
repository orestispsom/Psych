# ESL review contract

- Working title: ESL — extensive symptom list: existing foundations.
- Review question: Which verified inventories, empirical reorganizations and terminologies can support a categorized, source-linked inventory of psychiatric symptoms and signs across DSM-5-TR, ICD-10 and ICD-11?
- Intended reader: psychiatrist and project owner planning ESL.
- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-10-20.
- Unit of analysis: named inventory, taxonomy, terminology or primary study and its specific edition.
- Meaning of all: long-term coverage goal across declared sources; this initial review does not establish exhaustive coverage.

## Scope

- Include: primary research; originating organizations; official manuals and publisher previews; WHO and APA documentation; maintained terminology repositories.
- Exclude: unsourced lists; social media as evidence; diagnoses treated as symptoms; unverified bulk extractions.
- Date range: no lower limit; available by cutoff.
- Languages: English and German source verification; English synthesis.
- Known access limits: full DSM-5-TR and AMDP manuals not available in this review; not all instrument items or supplementary datasets inspected.

## Comparison axes

1. Inventory versus taxonomy versus terminology versus diagnostic reference.
2. Phenomenological granularity and diagnostic/source coverage.
3. Lifespan, setting and population limits.
4. Version-specific evidence, item access and reuse conditions.
5. Practical role in ESL and remaining verification work.
