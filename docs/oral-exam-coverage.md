# Oral exam coverage review

## Scope and source precedence

The four supplied Word documents are reference material, not instructions. **ΘΕΜΑΤΑ ΚΑΙ ΑΠΑΝΤΗΣΕΙΣ.docx** is the primary answer reference. The other three identify recalled questions and follow-up requirements. The app uses explanatory Greek study material, with topic sections and comparison tables, rather than first-person model responses. Examiner names and attributions are excluded.

The initial extraction contains 754 nonempty paragraphs from the first three documents and 13,700 from the primary reference. The draft inventory contains 1,711 *occurrences*: repeated prompts, headings and answer fragments, not 1,711 distinct examination questions. Repeated and bundled prompts are reconciled into the coverage groups below. A title in the primary reference does not establish that it was asked in an examination.

The “other necessary questions” section of document 2 (original paragraph 354 onward) is supplementary practice material. It is covered in chapter 6 but is not asserted to be a real-exam recollection. Bare question marks in document 1 (paragraphs 77, 79–81) contain no recoverable question and do not justify invented entries.

## Primary-reference navigation and corrections

The original OOXML paragraph numbers locate the principal reference blocks: psychopathology 0–669; assessment/classification/etiology/research 670–1495; stress/anxiety 1496–2965; depression 2966–4025; bipolar 4026–4599; schizophrenia 4600–5719; delusional disorders 5720–5945; eating/sleep/sexual topics 5946–6839; neurocognitive/neuropsychiatric topics 6840–8424; personality/child topics 8425–9370; old age 9531–9723; substances 9724–10696; suicide 10697–10991; liaison/perinatal material 10992–11335 and 14517–14888; psychotherapy 11361–11537; drugs/biological treatments 11538–14077; classification appendix 14081–14178; genetics 14179–14301; epigenetics 14488–14516; EEG 15390–15439; neurobiology/imaging 15440–15736.

Important corrections rather than literal import:

- ICD-10, ICD-11 and DSM editions are distinguished. A recalled inaccurate criterion is not treated as authoritative.
- Conflicting cognitive-deficit percentages are explained through population, instrument and threshold; no fabricated universal percentage replaces them.
- Heritability is distinct from recurrence risk. Single candidate genes and historical GWAS locus counts are not presented as diagnostic findings.
- Histone methylation is context dependent; acquired human epigenetic inheritance is not assumed.
- EEG and imaging associations are not individual diagnostic tests. rTMS uses motor-threshold calibration.
- Pregnancy does not automatically require antipsychotic dose reduction. Cannabis withdrawal does not routinely produce the seizure syndrome of alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal.
- Dementia screening cutoffs are context dependent. Suicide proportions among anorexia deaths are not described as the proportion of all patients who die by suicide.
- Catalepsy has no universal 60-second requirement; CJD, variant CJD and nonspecific neuronal-injury markers are distinguished.
- Clozapine monitoring reflects the revised European ANC framework; acute infection checks and safe dose re-titration remain separate obligations.

Raw extracts, lexical candidate matches and incomplete draft inventories are working files and are not shipped with the app. This crosswalk records topic coverage, not independent confirmation that every recollection occurred exactly as reported.
