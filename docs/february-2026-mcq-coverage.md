## Assessment Validity Analysis

**Assessment:** Single-best-answer Greek MCQs for psychiatry specialty revision.
**Claims to measure:** Recall of core concepts, diagnostic discrimination, interpretation of evidence and clinical decisions corresponding to the February recall topics.
**For:** Psychiatry trainees preparing for specialty examinations.

### Validity Analysis

**Construct validity:** Clinical diagnosis questions supply discriminating symptoms, duration and exclusions. Evidence questions name the study or guideline when a general claim would be ambiguous.
**Construct-irrelevant variance:** Edited unequal option lengths, categorical absolutes and implausible drug combinations where these could cue the key. Technical diagnosis names naturally vary in length.
**Construct underrepresentation:** Twenty-one original entries cannot be recovered faithfully; adaptations preserve useful study coverage while documenting changed targets. The MCMI item is thematic only.

### Reliability Analysis

Each new item has one keyed answer, five distinct alternatives and a rationale. This is an editorial assessment, not psychometric validation; item difficulty and distractor functioning require learner response data.

### Consequential Validity

Historical errors and unsupported prevalence/ranking claims are not reinforced. Source-specific findings are not presented as universal treatment rules or diagnostic biomarkers.

### Threats Identified

1. Informal recall contains missing options, contradictory keys and duplicate entries.
2. A broadly related study question can be mistaken for a recovered exam question.
3. Long or unusually qualified answers can reveal the key without clinical knowledge.

### Recommendations

1. Preserve the crosswalk’s exact-match/adaptation distinctions when reusing these questions.
2. Use the explanations and linked evidence to review disagreements rather than treating the recall as an authoritative key.
3. Revisit item difficulty and distractor performance when sufficient learner data is available.

### Overall Verdict

Suitable as an editorially reviewed revision-bank expansion. Not a verified reconstruction of the February examination or a psychometrically validated assessment.
