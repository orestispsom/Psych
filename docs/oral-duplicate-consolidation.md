## Decisions

The JSON manifest records each retired question and its live destination. Unique facts and caveats were carried over; numerical, treatment and legal anchors were preserved. Broad overview cards and focused follow-ups remain when they test a distinct decision, differential, safety step or definition. No unrelated clinical answer was rewritten.

Chapter 1: 23 → 12. Seven general catatonia cards consolidate into Q10; the separate differential absorbs the medication-mimic card. Detailed motor-sign definitions remain.

No consolidation in chapters 2, 12, 17, 18, 23 and 26. Empty chapters remain empty. Adult and childhood developmental assessments were not merged across different populations. Hospitalization criteria, legal process, documentation and communication remain separate. Medication monitoring, toxicity management and mechanism questions remain separate where their answers serve different tasks.

All source records and historical checkmarks remain available in stored data. Old links redirect to a live destination with no redirect chains. A historical check does not automatically mark the broader replacement complete.

Total: 491 → 407 active questions; 84 cards consolidated.
