# English Б├■ Greek Psychiatry Translation Guide v3

Comprehensive bilingual terminology authority for Greek Adult Psychiatry board preparation, oral examinations, mental-state examination and clinical documentation, neuropsychiatry/neuroanatomy, and educational-product translation.

## How to use this guide

- **Preferred Greek** is the house form to use by default in Greek answers and documents.
- **Accepted alternative / historical term** is for recognition, searchability, or context-specific use; it is not automatically co-preferred.
- **Usage notes** appear only where they prevent a clinically meaningful confusion.
- Distinct constructs remain on distinct rows even when older Greek teaching sometimes used one shorthand for both.
- Neuroanatomy is organised for psychiatric retrieval: cortex and lobes, limbic/subcortical structures, basal ganglia, thalamus/hypothalamus, monoaminergic systems and major white-matter pathways.

**Column order:** Preferred Greek | English | Accepted alternative / historical term | Usage note
